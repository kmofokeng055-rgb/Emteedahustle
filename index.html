01 — THE STORY

FROM SOWETO TO THE WORLD

BUILT FROM
THE GROUND UP.

Mthembeni Ndevu, professionally known as Emtee, is a South African rapper, singer, songwriter and record producer celebrated for helping popularise African trap music.

He rose to prominence with Roll Up in 2015, followed by his platinum-certified debut album Avery. His catalogue includes Manando, DIY 2, Logan and DIY 3. He later established Emtee Records and continued building his independent musical journey.

Born 17 September 1992 · 34 years old in 2026. This is an independent fan portfolio; biographical details are presented for music-discovery purposes.
